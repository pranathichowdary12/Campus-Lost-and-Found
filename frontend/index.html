About Our Portal

Campus Lost and Found is designed to help the Sai University community manage lost and found belongings easily. Students can report items, search for belongings and submit claims through one centralized platform.

What Can You Do?

🔍

Find Lost Items

Search for belongings reported by other students using item names, categories and locations.

📢

Report Found Items

Report items you have found on campus and help their owners recover them.

📦

Track Your Claims

Submit ownership claims and check whether they are pending, approved or rejected.
